It was decided to keep it very simple with a rectangle of 3 different colors, R,G, and B.
The black canvas is to visibly show the limitations of the avatar's range of motion, so
there is no confusion as to why the avatar cannot move any further.
There is a description on how to move the avatar just above the canvas, so the user knows how
to move the avatar around, and what can be done to the avatar altogether.

A moves it to the left, W moves it up, S moves it down, and D moves it to the right. 1 resets
the avatar back to the origin.
